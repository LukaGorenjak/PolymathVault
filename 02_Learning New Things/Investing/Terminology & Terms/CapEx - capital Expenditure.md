# What is is?
It is the money a business spends to buy, maintain or improve long-term physical or intangible assets - such as building, machinery, vehicles, land, software.

# Key Charactheristics
- **Long-term value:** the asset provides a benefit to the company for more than a year
- **Balance sheet treatment:** Instead of being deducted immediately as a single-year cost, CapEx is recorded as an asset and its cost is spread out over time through **depreciation** or **amortization**.
- **Growth or maintenance focus:** Companies use **CapEx** either to keep existing operations running (maintenance) or to expand and grow the business (growth

# Common Examples
- Purchasing a new factory, warehouse, or office building.
- Buying heavy machinery, production equipment, or office computers.
- Upgrading a roof or repaving a parking lot to extend the useful life of a property.
- Acquiring long-term software platforms, patents, or copyrights.
