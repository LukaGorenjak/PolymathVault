
# What is it?
It is how much profit a company makes for each outstanding share over a specific period.

# Usage
- Used to gauge profitability, compare companies and calculate ratios like the P/E ration.
-  In earning reports missing a reported quarterly EPS pushes down the stock, while beating it lifts it. 
- EPS can be increased through stock buybacks

# Formulas and Types
### Basic
- *(Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding*
- Preferred dividends are subtracted because they belong to preferred shareholders rather than common stockholders.
