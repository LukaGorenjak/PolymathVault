Euclidean Distance is the length between two points in a plane or a higher dimensional space.

**Formula:**
$$d(p, q) = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (q_i - p_i)^2}$$
**Uses:**
- **Geometry & Physics:** Measuring real-world physical lengths and coordinates.
- **Machine Learning:** Powering proximity-based algorithms like [[K-Nearest Neighbors]] (k-NN) and [[K-means Clustering]].
- **GIS Mapping:** Finding straight-line proximity to nearest locations or targets in spatial analysis.
