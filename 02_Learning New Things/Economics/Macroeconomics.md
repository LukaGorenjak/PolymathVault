==🟢Is a branch of **economics** that studies the **structure**, **performance**, **behavior** and **decision-making** of an economy as a **whole**, rather than on an **individual or single-market level**. ==

==Or simply said study of the entire economy as a big picture, rather than individual people or single businesses. Mainly **regional, national and global economies.**==

# Core Focus and Variables of Macroeconomics
- **[[Gross Domestic Product (GDP)]]** 
- **[[Inflation and Deflation]]**
- **[[Unemployment]]**
- Economic Growth
- Balance of Trade
- Consumption
- Investment
- [[Business Cycle]]
Macroeconomists primarily focus on questions that help to understand aggregate variables (combined/summarized grouping of data points) in relation to long-run **economic growth**.

# How can macroeconomics be divided?
1. Time Frames:
	- The Short Run (a few years)
	- The Medium Run (a decade)
	- The Long Run (a couple of decades or more)
2. Economy openness:
	 - Closed economies
	 - Open economies
# Main Policy Tools
Governments and central banks use macroeconomic principles to stabilize and manage the business cycle:
- **Fiscal Policy**
- **[[Monetary Policy]]**

 ### How to Build Functional Macro Literacy Quickly

You do not need an advanced academic degree to profit from macroeconomic literacy. Focus on understanding these core relationships:

1. **The Business Cycle:** Learn how economies expand, peak, contract, and recover over time.
    
2. **Central Banks & Liquidity:** Track how interest rates and balance sheet policies influence the flow of money in financial markets.
    
3. **Core Economic Indicators:** Follow basic releases like Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Consumer Price Index (CPI), and employment reports to see how the overall machine is running.
