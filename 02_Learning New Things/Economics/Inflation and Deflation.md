==🟢A general **price increase** across the entire economy is called **inflation**. When it **decreases**, there is **deflation**. The changes in prices are measured with **[[price indexes]]**. Inflation will **rise** when an economy **overheats** and **grows too quickly**. Same with deflation and **opposite**.==

Changes in inflation rate may result from several factors. 
- Too much [[Aggregate Demand (AD)]] will cause an **overheating**, **raising inflation rates** via the **Phillips curve** because of a tight **labor market** leading to large **wage increases** which are transmitted to **increase the price of the products of employers.**
- **Aggregate Supply Shocks** (oil crises of the 70s, 2021-23 global energy crisis).
- Also impacts the formation of **inflation expectations**, creating a **self-fulfilling in / deflationary spiral**.

==🔴But the monetarist **Quantity Theory of Money (QTM)** holds that **changes in price levels** are directly caused by **changes in the money supply**. The **central's bankers** conducting [[Monetary Policy]] main priority is to **avoid high inflation** (typically by adjusting **interest rates**), rather a **positive and stable** one (reduced **uncertainty**). ==
