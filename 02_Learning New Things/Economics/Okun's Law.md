Is an empirically observed **relationship between unemployment and looses in a country's production**. Named after **Arthur Melvin Okun**, who proposed it in **1962**.

The **"gap version"** states that for every 1% increase in the unemployment rate, a country's GDP will experience a roughly 2% decrease on its potential GDP.

The **"difference version"** describes the relationship between quarterly changes in unemployment and quarterly changes in real GDP. 

The stability and usefulness of the law has been disputed.
