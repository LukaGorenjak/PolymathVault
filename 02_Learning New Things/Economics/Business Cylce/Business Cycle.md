==🟢Intervals of general **expansion** followed by **[[Recession]]** in economic performance. These episodes typically **last from 2 to 10 years** and **varying intensity** over time.==

![[Pasted image 20261023223156.png]]

These changes have important implications for the welfare of the general population, government, institutions, and private sector firms.

Many definitions of a business cycle:
- **The simplest**: 2 consecutive quarters of negative GDP growth
- **Better**: A full picture considers more than just GDP and looks for broader trends across the economy
	==U.S. definition, NBER defines it as a significant drop in activity lasting more than a few months. 5 key areas:
	1. Real GDP
	2. Real Income
	3. Employment
	4. Industrial Production 
	5. Wholesale & Retail Sales

Are usually thought of as medium-term phenomenon. Less related to long-term trends from slowly changing factors (tech advances). An unusual one-period change in a course of a year or two is often regarded as noise (worker strike or isolated period of severe weather).
