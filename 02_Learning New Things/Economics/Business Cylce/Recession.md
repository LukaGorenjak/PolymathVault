# Why do recessions occur?
Recessions generally occur when there is a **widespread drop in spending** (an adverse [[Demand Shock]]). This may be **triggered** by events such as a **financial crisis**, **external trade shock**, an adverse [[Supply Shock]], the bursting of a **[[Economic Bubble]]** or a **large scale natural disaster.**

# Responses to recessions
Governments respond by adopting expansionary macroeconomic policies, such as increasing money supply and decreasing interest rates or increasing government spending and decreasing taxation.
