==🟢It is measured by the **unemployment rate** (the percentage of persons in the **[[Labor Force]]** who do not have a job, but are **actively looking for one**). ==

Unemployment has a **short-run cyclical component** which depends on the **[[Business Cycle]]**, and a more **permanent structural component**, which can be thought of as the **average unemployment rate** in an economy over extended periods, often termed **natural or structural rate of unemployment.**

**[[Cyclical Unemployment]]** occurs when growth **stagnates**. **[[Okun's Law]]** represents the relationship between unemployment and short-run GDP growth.

The [[Structural or Natural Rate of Unemployment]] is the level of unemployment that will occur in a **medium-run equilibrium**, a situation with a **cyclical unemployment rate of zero**. There may be reasons why there is some positive unemployment rate even in a neutral situation:
- Search unemployment or Frictional unemployment
- Efficiency wage
- Trade unions
- Legal minimum wages
